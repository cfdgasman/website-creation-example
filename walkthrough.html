01

Find a candidate in the Screener

Layer 2 — universe and setup preconditions. This is where a session starts: not "is Example good," but "which of 529 names even clear the bar today."

Open the Screener (L2) tab and pick a watchlist or filter — My watchlist below. Each candidate is scored against a fixed checklist grouped into four categories. A name that clears every automatable box shows QUALIFIES · 12/12; a manual-only box (optionable, earnings distance, pending M&A) is marked with a dot, never a check — the desk can't see it from a price feed, so it never silently counts it as a pass.

    Quick glossary

    Badges and terms that recur across every tab.
    Confidence: high / partial / nodata
    How much of the composite's usual evidence was actually measurable — not how strong the signal is.
    historical data vs Nowcast vs market tape
    Three different clocks: quarterly and disclosed, modelled since the quarter closed, and live price/volume.
    Confirmed vs Unbacked advance
    Confirmed means price and flow agree; Unbacked means price is rising without the tape paying for it.
    TESTING a level
    Always instructs waiting — a level state is decided on closes, never on a wick.
    NEEDS YOUR EYES
    The Playbook's ceiling for any setup — some conditions (CVD, footprint) can't be scored from daily OHLCV.
    R-multiple
    Profit or loss measured against the original risk at entry, not the trailed stop that may have actually closed the trade.

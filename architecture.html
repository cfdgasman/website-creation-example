01 — Foundation

Core Architecture

Eight-layer decision framework. Each layer builds on the previous, from market regime through position-level mechanics — so a ticker that fails an early layer never wastes time in a later one.

Solaris Flow implements a complete decision system organized as eight discrete layers. Each one answers a specific question, in order, so traders can quickly rule out tickers that fail early criteria before investing time in deeper analysis.

Layer 0: Regime

Market structure, sector rotation, and breadth. Is the current regime suitable for your system's edge?

Layer 1: Selection

Correlation caps and portfolio-level filters. Does this name fit your current exposure profile?

Layer 2: Universe

Technical preconditions and trading profile. Does this ticker pass the automatable setup checklist? — see the Screener.

Layer 3: Playbook

Level interaction and price action. What should you do now that price is at a level? — see the Level Playbook.

Layer 4: Backtest

Historical setup performance. Does your signal have positive expectancy over the past 3–5 years? — see Backtesting.

Layer 5: Management

Position sizing and exit rules. What's your risk per trade and how do you manage winners?

Layer 6: Checklist

Pre-trade confirmation. Ten binary gates: optionable, earnings distance, bid-ask spread, etc.

Layer 7: Journal

Trade recording and expectancy analysis. How did your actual trades perform against the plan?

Why this structure?

The eight layers work as a filter: Layer 0 eliminates days when regime is broken; Layer 2 eliminates tickers that don't meet setup criteria; Layer 3 eliminates times when price is not near a level; Layer 4 eliminates setups without edge. By the time you reach Layer 6, you've already ruled out 90% of possibilities — the checklist items are binary confirmations of a trade you've already decided is worth the work.

Where each layer lives in the desk

Layers aren't abstract — each maps to a concrete screen or file in the actual application:

  • Layer 0 (Regime) — market-breadth indices tab + sector rotation dashboard in index.html
  • Layer 1 (Selection) — correlation cap logic, portfolio-level risk board
  • Layer 2 (Universe) — the Screener tab, scored by server/flow/analytics.js
  • Layer 3 (Playbook) — the Level Playbook tab, powered by flow/levels.js + flow/playbook.js
  • Layer 4 (Backtest) — the Backtest tab, replaying Setup A/B/C over stored bars
  • Layer 5 (Management) — the position-size calculator in tool/index.html
  • Layer 6 (Checklist) — the 10-point pre-trade checklist, also in tool/index.html
  • Layer 7 (Journal) — the trade journal with expectancy breakdowns and circuit-breaker flags
Two codebases, one set of decision engines. The Layer Desk (layers/index.html) and its server-synced twin (server/public/layers.html) share identical decision logic — only the log-handling functions differ, so the audit trail can persist across devices without forking the rules themselves.

02 — Discovery · Layer 2

The Screener

Universe and setup preconditions. Find candidates that clear the automatable bar before drilling into individual charts.

The Screener is the entry point: sort 500+ tickers by preset filters or create your own watchlist, then see which names qualify against four categories of rules.

Built-in filters

  • My watchlist — custom selection from portfolio, notes, or manual entry
  • Candidates in leading sectors — XLS, XLE, XLV, etc., ranked in top 3 of 11 sectors
  • In accumulation — institutional net buying over last 90 days (historical data + nowcast)
  • Discount (VWAP) — price below the daily VWAP over last 20 sessions
  • Close to VAL — price within one ATR of the volume-area low
  • Near earnings dates — >7 sessions from report day (setup gate)

Real screenshot — Screener, Example qualify card

Four-category qualification

Universe

Liquid, tradeable names. Price >$10, 20d avg volume >$25M, optionable, earnings >7 sessions out.

Relative Strength

Outperforms S&P 500 over 63d, outperforms sector over 21d, sector ranked top-3.

Trend Structure

Price >200DMA, 200DMA flat/rising, 50DMA >200DMA. Classic uptrend alignment.

Contraction

ATR compressed vs own 50d avg, range tightening, consolidation volume below 20d average.

Manual vs automatic gates. Optionable, earnings distance, and M&A status can't be scored from a price feed — they're marked with a dot, not a checkmark. A name missing one or two automatable boxes still qualifies as "Close" and is worth a second look.
  • 18 of 530 typical candidates pass every automatable box; 154 miss by just one or two.
  • Passing the Screener means Layer 2 is clear — not that you should trade it yet; Layer 3 setup quality still matters.
  • Clicking a candidate drills into Ticker Detail, where the institutional flow analysis happens.

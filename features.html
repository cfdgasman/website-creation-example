Trade Journal (Layer 7)

Record every trade with setup type, entry, exit, R-multiple, and expected outcome. Journal computes:

  • Expectancy realized — what you actually earned vs what the backtest said you should earn
  • Win/loss streaks — identifies tilt and discipline gaps
  • Circuit breaker flags — auto-stops if losses exceed daily/weekly/monthly cap
  • R-curve histogram — visual distribution of outcomes to spot clustering

Position Sizing Calculator

Given account size, risk per trade (%), and stop distance, calculates position size automatically. Enforces:

  • Correlation caps — no single sector >30% of open risk
  • Position caps — no single trade >20% of account
  • Daily max loss — typically 2–3% per day; stop trading after that
  • R-denominator — all position math in R-units, not dollars, for consistency

Pre-trade Checklist (Layer 6)

Ten binary gates to confirm before sizing:

  • Earnings >7 sessions away
  • Bid-ask spread <0.1%
  • 20d avg volume sufficient for exit
  • Stop placement confirmed on chart
  • Position size calculated and capped
  • No sector concentration overweight
  • Risk-reward ratio >1.5:1
  • Circuit breaker flags clear
  • Narrative on Ticker Detail positive
  • Setup on chart manually confirmed (manual market checks)

Portfolio-level Risk Dashboard (Layer 0)

Real-time view of open risk by position, by sector, and by circuit-breaker threshold. Shows:

  • Open risk / max risk — how much of your daily loss cap is already at stake
  • Sector caps — Health Care cannot exceed 30% of open risk
  • Correlation overlay — flag if two open positions are >0.8 correlated
  • Stop-out prices — list of each position's exit triggers

Seasonality — calendar months, sector rotation, earnings days

Every sector ETF's average excess return over SPX, month by month — which sleeve has historically led each month, with previous/current/next laid out side by side so you can see whether a rotation is continuing or reversing.

Real screenshot — Seasonality → Sector rotation

  • Calendar months — compounded seasonal windows per ticker across its full history
  • Sector rotation — leaders/laggards per month, ranked by beat-rate and sample size (n)
  • Earnings days — historical price behavior around a ticker's own report day
Sector histories differ in length — XLK/XLF/XLV/XLY/XLP/XLE/XLI/XLB/XLU run back to 1999, XLRE only to 2015, XLC only to 2018. Months with fewer than 5 observations are excluded from the rankings, and a short-history sector's "lead" rests on far fewer years than XLK or XLF — the tab says this explicitly rather than letting a thin sample look as confident as a deep one.

Trading Scripts (18 indicators)

Complete chart toolkit for every layer of the system:

  • Core — Moving averages, VWAP, ATR, RSI
  • Trend confirmation — EMA ribbon, slope detection, trend direction signals
  • Momentum — MACD, Stochastic, Bollinger Bands, volume oscillators
  • Market structure — Swing highs/lows, BOS (break of structure), ChoCh (change of character)
  • Flow signals — Chaikin Money Flow, accumulation/distribution days
  • Setup scanner — Detects A/B/C setups live, marks them on the chart
  • Regime dashboard — Market breadth, VIX, sector rotation, internals
  • Portfolio risk board — Position caps, sector limits, circuit-breaker status
Never hardcode hex colors. All 18 scripts source one color palette from COLOR-SYSTEM.md. To change the desk's look, update that one file and recompile the scripts. A single source of truth prevents color drift between chart and app.

Mobile & Standalone (iPhone)

A self-contained HTML file that runs the full Screener, Ticker Detail, and Backtest on your phone, offline:

  • historical data snapshot baked in — updated weekly; no live historical data from mobile (quarterly data doesn't change daily)
  • Price feed from market data — CORS-enabled, works from a browser on LAN or WiFi
  • Add to Home Screen — PWA installer; runs full-screen without browser chrome
  • Offline journal — localStorage replay; syncs to server when you're back online
  • Always edit tools/standalone-template.html and run npm run build:phone — standalone/index.html is generated.
  • Phone has its own retry queue (localStorage, 8 req/min market data free tier) with the same probe logic.
